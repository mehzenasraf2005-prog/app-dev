## My Favorite Series: The Penthouse: War in Life

> *"Greed has no limits when you live at the very top."*

**The Penthouse: War in Life** is a South Korean suspense drama television series that revolves around the wealthy residents of *Hera Palace*, a luxury 100-floor penthouse apartment, and the fierce competition at *Cheong-ah Arts High School*.

### Why I Love This Series:
* **Intense Plot Twists:** Every episode ends with shocking betrayals and unexpected revelations.
* **Powerful Acting:** Led by an iconic cast including Lee Ji-ah, Kim So-yeon, and Eugene.
* **Dramatic Themes:** Explores ambition, revenge, social status, and how far parents will go for their children.

### Top 3 Most Iconic Characters:
1. **Cheon Seo-jin** – The ruthless and ambitious soprano diva.
2. **Shim Su-ryeon** – The elegant and strategic queen of Hera Palace.
3. **Oh Yoon-hee** – A mother willing to sacrifice everything to enter high society.
